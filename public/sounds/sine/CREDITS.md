# Sounds

The UI sounds in this folder are the **Sine** kit (kit 01) of **SND**, by Dentsu Lab Tokyo
(https://snd.dev/), taken from the `snd-lib` package (v1.2.4).

SND's sounds may be used free of charge, commercially or not. Copyright and all other
intellectual property rights in the audio files belong to the credited sound designer.
See https://snd.dev/ for the terms of use.

`audioSprite.json` is the sprite map: where each sound starts and ends in the audio file.
